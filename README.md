## John Khan

I build data tools for Canadian borrowers, homeowners and lenders. I've spent about 20 years in mortgages, business lending and alternative investments, and I'm finishing an MS in Data Science at the University of Colorado Boulder with a Graduate Certificate in AI. Based in Richmond Hill, Ontario.

### Projects

| Project | What it does | Live |
|---|---|---|
| [Canadian Mortgage Calculator Suite](https://github.com/ALENZACODES/canadian-mortgage-suite) | Fourteen calculators built on 2026 OSFI, CMHC and land transfer tax rules, each with a cited source. 129 tests. | [Open](https://alenzacodes.github.io/canadian-mortgage-suite/) |
| [Renewal Check](https://github.com/ALENZACODES/renewal-check) | Your new payment at renewal, what keeping your current payment does, and the cost of breaking early. English and French. | [Open](https://alenzacodes.github.io/renewal-check/) |
| [Canada's Rate Cycle](https://github.com/ALENZACODES/canada-rate-cycle-dashboard) | Bank of Canada policy rate, inflation, mortgage rates and USD/CAD, 2015 to 2026. | [Open](https://alenzacodes.github.io/canada-rate-cycle-dashboard/) |
| [Quiet Charges](https://github.com/ALENZACODES/quiet-charges) | Finds the subscriptions in a bank export and the ones that quietly raised their price. Runs in the browser. | [Open](https://alenzacodes.github.io/quiet-charges/) |
| [Loan File Pre-Underwriting Check](https://github.com/ALENZACODES/pre-underwriting-copilot) | Catches the problems that get a mortgage file bounced: ratios at the qualifying rate, short down payments, stale documents. | [Open](https://alenzacodes.github.io/pre-underwriting-copilot/) |
| [Private Credit Deal Screener](https://github.com/ALENZACODES/private-credit-screener) | Grades a senior real estate loan on LTV, DSCR, exit LTV and debt yield, and drafts a credit memo. | [Open](https://alenzacodes.github.io/private-credit-screener/) |
| [Mortgage Decision Lab](https://github.com/ALENZACODES/mortgage-decision-lab) | Stress test, renewal, rent vs buy, prepayment, penalty, and a Canadian mortgage beside a US 30-year fixed. | [Open](https://alenzacodes.github.io/mortgage-decision-lab/) |
| [Renewal Shock Radar](https://github.com/ALENZACODES/renewal-shock-radar) | The national renewal wave from CMHC data, and what it means for one household's payment. | [Open](https://alenzacodes.github.io/renewal-shock-radar/) |

Everything runs in the browser, uses public data from the Bank of Canada, Statistics Canada, CMHC and OSFI, and links to its sources.
